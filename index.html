Chainbreaker

Earn your scroll time.

A calm app blocker for Android. Time on the apps that help you earns time on the ones that pull you in.

How it works

  • Pick your productive apps and your distracting apps.
  • Set your own trade, for example 60 minutes of focus earns 10 minutes of scroll.
  • When your free time is used up, the distracting apps wait.
  • Each good day adds a link to your chain. One off day won't break it.

Everything stays on your phone. Chainbreaker has no internet access, no accounts, no ads and no analytics.
